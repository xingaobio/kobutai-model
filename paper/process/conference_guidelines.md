# Conference Guidelines

Target: **arXiv preprint** (categories cs.CL primary, cs.LG / cs.IR secondary). Not a double-blind venue; author information will be added by the author after drafting, so drafts stay anonymous.

- **Page limit:** 8 pages of main text, single column, 11 pt; references and appendices do not count.
- **Mandatory sections:** Abstract, Introduction, Related Work, Method, Experimental Setup, Results, Analysis / Negative Results, Limitations, Conclusion. A short "Disclosure of AI assistance" paragraph before the references is required, stating how AI tools were used (the experiments and code were carried out by an AI coding agent directed by the author; training data, synthetic evaluation sets and the labels of the real-world set were produced by large language models).
- **Formatting:** single-column `article`, 1-inch margins, natbib author–year citations, booktabs tables, figures as PNG or PDF at column width.
- **Artefacts:** the model, code, report and demo are public; the paper may say "released openly" but must not include URLs in the anonymous draft (the author will add them).
- **Submission date:** 2026-10-20. Literature cutoff: 2026-10-01.
- **Tone:** a short empirical paper; claims must be backed by the experimental log; report confidence intervals and be explicit about the small test set and LLM-produced labels.
